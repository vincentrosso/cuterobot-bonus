Checklist — top to bottom, no skipping

Two fixed rules, every bet: put the underdog on the promo book, put the hedge on the favorite at a different book. Big liquid games only (MLB, NBA, World Cup). The tracker gives you the exact hedge dollar from the real odds — the amounts here assume roughly +150 / −180 and round up.

Signup links are each book's Colorado page — verify the offer on arrival, they rotate. Check boxes save in this browser.

Timing — space the two trips 6–10 days apart.
  • Wait at least 3 days after the qualifiers settle before going back — bonuses credit on a lag (BetMGM up to 72h after the bet loses).
  • Be back within ~7 days of qualifying — credited bonus bets usually expire in 7 days (varies by book — write each expiry date in the tracker). Caesars boost tokens: hard 14-day limit.
  • 6–10 days is the working window. Every bet, including the conversions, must be placed while physically in Colorado.
  • Doing it as one stay instead: qualify day 1 on games that settle that night, convert days 2–3.
  • FanDuel (7-day) and Fanatics (10-day) need consecutive in-state days — miss one and the streak resets. Skip unless you're staying.
