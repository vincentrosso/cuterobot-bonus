Sportsbook bonus extraction — my notes

Personal tool. California has no legal online sportsbook, so the play is: drive to a legal state, claim each book's welcome bonus once, and hedge every bet across two books so the outcome is locked in regardless of who wins. Legal, openly taught (DarkHorse Odds, ProfitDuel, OddsJam), and strictly one-time per person — welcome bonuses don't repeat.

Honest reality check

Solo, one working day

~$1,500–1,700 net

Skip the multi-day promos. After CA tax (~9.3% marginal, worldwide income, credit for tax paid to the betting state) — and pending the itemization question above.

With multi-day promos (2 trips)

~$2,200–2,500 net

FanDuel's 7-day and Fanatics' 10-day need consecutive in-state days, so this means a longer stay or a second trip.

BetMGM, honestly. Hedging the qualifying bet costs money, so the guaranteed floor is roughly $450–750. The ~$1,050 figure only lands in the branch where the first bet loses and the full bonus triggers. The tracker shows both branches.

Day one is not payday. Bonus bets credit only after the qualifying bet settles; a second in-state hedged bet then converts them to cash. A noon arrival gets registration, KYC, qualifiers, and maybe the one instant DraftKings conversion — real cash day-one is more like $100–150. The rest needs trip two.

This is not a business. One-time cash grab. Multi-person only adds refer-a-friend bonuses (~$350–700/person), and everyone runs their own independent accounts — no pooling, no splitting, no coordination.

Guardrails

  • Never bet both sides on the same book — that's the one pattern that gets bonuses voided.
  • Stick to big liquid markets (MLB, World Cup, NBA). Obscure props draw flags.
  • Banking: Chime / prepaid gets rejected by many books. Use Ally or a normal bank debit; unlock gambling MCC codes if the bank blocks them.
  • Worst case is a confiscated bonus and wasted time — not lost capital. Books can limit accounts or void bonuses per their T&Cs (Caesars explicitly bars "exploitative" patterns).
  • Float: ~$1,500 solo captures BetMGM if sized down; ~$3,000 for full-size simultaneous bets. Money returns as bets settle (24–72 hrs). Test run: $1,000, 6 books, sequence BetMGM after BetRivers settles.
