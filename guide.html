The hedge, in one paragraph

Every play is two straight bets on the same game at two different books. Put the underdog on the promo book — you want the qualifier to lose so the "money back" bonus triggers. Hedge the favorite on a second book. Size the stakes so both outcomes land within ~$50 of each other. When the bonus credits, repeat the move to convert bonus bets to cash: bonus bet on a big underdog (+200 to +300 converts best), cash hedge on the favorite elsewhere. DarkHorse Odds' Bonus Bet Finder computes these pairs; the tracker replicates the math so it doesn't depend on a subscription.

Why it works

Convert each book's American odds to decimal: d = 1 + (odds/100) for positive, d = 1 + (100/|odds|) for negative. A stake S on the promo book at decimal dA, hedged with stake H at dB on the other side:

If the promo side winsS·(dA−1) − H
If the promo side loses−S + H·(dB−1) + (bonus × conversion rate)

For a plain "money back if it loses" offer, set H = (S·dA − bonus·rate) / dB and both branches net the same — that equal number is your guaranteed floor. For a bonus-bet conversion (stake not returned), set H = S·(dA−1) / dB. Conversion rate on a bonus bet is typically 0.66–0.72.

Trip 1 — Denver, arrival day

  1. Bank check before you leave. Confirm your debit card is a normal bank card (Ally or similar — not Chime/prepaid) and that gambling MCC codes aren't blocked. Call the bank if unsure.
  2. Cross into CO, confirm geolocation. Each app checks your location on registration and on every bet. Denver is well inside the boundary; don't try to register from the I-70 shoulder.
  3. Register + KYC every book. Real name, SSN last-4, address. Do all nine majors plus Circa / SuperBook / Betfred. Use referral links between your own accounts only if you have a second person — never self-refer.
  4. Deposit the minimum each book needs (see table below). BetMGM and the $500 second-chance books are the float-heavy ones — sequence BetMGM after BetRivers settles if you're running the $1,000 test float.
  5. Place qualifying bets on same-day games. Underdog on the promo book, favorite hedge on another book, stakes from the calculator. MLB afternoon games and live World Cup (through July 19, 2026) settle same-day.
  6. Do the one instant conversion: DraftKings. Bet $5, get $200 in bonus bets immediately, hedge a +200–300 underdog with them right away.
  7. Log every leg in the tracker as you go — book, side, odds, stake, hedge. The parking lot is the worst place to reconstruct this later.

Trip 2 — any CO stop, 1–2 weeks later

  1. Convert credited bonus bets. For each book with bonus bets sitting in the balance: bonus bet on a big underdog (+200 to +300), cash hedge on the favorite at another book. The calculator's "bonus conversion" mode sizes the hedge.
  2. Spend Caesars profit-boost tokens. They expire 14 days after issue. Use them on near-even games, hedged.
  3. Withdraw everything. Back to the same funding source. Expect 1–5 business days per book.
  4. Reconcile the tracker. Fill in cashRealized and grossWinnings per row. Gross winnings is the tax number — every winning leg, not the net.

Per-book SOP

Book-specific notes

  • BetMGM — bet the underdog. Slowest to credit (up to 72h). Full $1,500 version needs CO/AZ/OH/NC; it drops to ~$150 in PA/NJ/MI/WV/NV/NY.
  • Fanatics / FanDuel — multi-day (10 and 7 consecutive in-state days). Skip on a test run; only worth it on an extended stay or trip two.
  • bet365 — $10 stake is small enough that no hedge is needed on the qualifier. Just take the $365 in bonus bets and convert on trip two.
  • Caesars — the $1 bet is trivial; the value is the 10 profit-boost tokens. 14-day expiry, $25 max boost each. Convert on trip two.
  • Hard Rock Bet — "double winnings on first 10 bets", $50 max each. Verify CO availability specifically — its state list is narrower than the others.
  • DraftKings — the proof-of-loop book. Instant credit, so it's the one full loop you can close on day one.
  • ESPN BET / BetRivers — standard bet-and-get / second-chance. Underdog on the promo side.

Game selection rules

  • Any game listed on both books works. Tighter odds = smaller hedge cost = more profit. The tool picks the tightest pair.
  • Qualifiers want the tightest available odds. Conversions want a big underdog (+200–300).
  • Must settle same-day if you're converting on the same trip — MLB afternoons, live World Cup.
  • Avoid low-liquidity markets (table tennis, obscure props). Wide spreads plus weird staking patterns is exactly what triggers account flags.
