## Corrected facts (mistakes caught in planning — bake these in)

1. **Float floor:** ~$1,500 solo captures BetMGM if sized down; $3,000 for full-size + simultaneous bets. Float recycles (bets settle 24-72 hrs, money returns). Test run: $1,000, 6 books, sequence BetMGM after BetRivers settles.
2. **CA taxes worldwide income** (~9.3% marginal), credit for tax paid to the betting state. Earlier nets were overstated.
3. **TAX — BLOCKING ISSUE:** IRS taxes GROSS winnings as income; losses (the losing hedge leg) deduct only on Schedule A if itemizing. Standard deduction → tax on gross wins with no offset → can eat 30-40% of profit or worse. Vincent has a CPA — get the answer before real money. Tracker must have a gross-winnings column and a pinned banner about this.
4. **Same-day completion isn't possible.** Bonus bets credit only after the qualifying bet settles, then a second in-state hedged bet converts them to cash. A noon-arrival day gets registration + qualifiers + maybe the one instant DK conversion. Real cash day-one ≈ $100-150; rest needs trip two. Don't promise "walk out with $600 today."
5. **Banking:** Chime/prepaid rejected by many books — use Ally or a normal bank debit. Unlock gambling MCC codes if the bank blocks them.
6. **Books can void bonuses / limit accounts** per their T&Cs (Caesars explicitly bars "exploitative" patterns). Downside is a confiscated bonus + wasted time, not lost capital. Never bet both sides on the SAME book. Stick to big liquid markets (MLB, World Cup, NBA) — obscure props draw flags.

## The hedge, in one paragraph (for the guide page)

Every example is: one side on the book with the promo, the other side on a different book, same game. Put the underdog on the promo book (you WANT the qualifier to lose so the "money back" bonus triggers). Hedge the favorite on a second book. Size stakes so both outcomes land within ~$50 of each other. When the bonus credits, repeat the move to convert bonus bets to cash — bonus bet on a big underdog (+200 to +300 converts best), cash hedge on the favorite elsewhere. DarkHorse Odds' Bonus Bet Finder computes these pairs; the site's tracker replicates the math so it's not dependent on a subscription.

## Game selection rules (for the guide)

- Any game listed on both books works. Tighter odds = smaller hedge cost = more profit. Tool picks the tightest pair.
- Must settle same-day if converting on the same trip (MLB afternoons, live World Cup through July 19 2026).
- Conversions want a big underdog (+200-300). Qualifiers want tightest available.
- Avoid low-liquidity markets (table tennis, obscure props) — wide spreads + weird patterns = account flags.
